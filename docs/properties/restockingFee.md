# restockingFee

Use [[MonetaryAmount]] to specify a fixed restocking fee for product returns, or use [[Number]] to specify a percentage of the product price paid by the customer.

**Domain**: [MerchantReturnPolicy](../classes/MerchantReturnPolicy.md), [MerchantReturnPolicySeasonalOverride](../classes/MerchantReturnPolicySeasonalOverride.md)

**Range**: [MonetaryAmount](../classes/MonetaryAmount.md), [Number](../classes/Number.md)
